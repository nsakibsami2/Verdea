A Botanical Idea

VERDÉA started with a simple question: why should a beautiful fragrance be fragile, bulky or reserved for special occasions? Inspired by flowers, citrus groves and warm woods, we imagined perfume you can slip into a handbag and touch onto skin in seconds.

Built around a plant-derived base, our solid perfume is made to feel premium without feeling out of reach.

Beginner’s guide

Why Solid Perfume?

A simple introduction to a compact, easy way to wear fragrance.

What is solid perfume?

Solid perfume is fragrance in a balm-like form, kept in a small tin. Instead of misting it into the air, you touch a little onto your skin.

Why portability matters

A flat, compact tin slips into a pocket, pencil case or handbag. It’s easy to carry through the day and simple to reapply whenever you like.

Solid vs. spray

Solid perfumeSpray perfume
FormatBalm in a small tinLiquid in a bottle
ApplicationTouch on with a fingertipMist from a distance
CarryingSlim and pocket-friendlyOften larger and more fragile

How to apply

Open

Twist or lift the lid of your tin.

Touch

Warm a fingertip against the surface.

Apply

Dab on pulse points like wrists or neck.

Carry

Close the tin and take it with you.

Everyone’s skin and scent experience is different. Do a small patch test if you have sensitive skin.
